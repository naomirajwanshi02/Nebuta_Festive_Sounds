# Sources

Facts in `js/data.js`:

- Festival: Aomori, early August (2–7 August), parade of lit paper floats, haneto dancers
  shouting "Rassera", Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property (1980); origin linked to Tanabata.
  - [Aomori Tourism](https://aomori-tourism.com/en/spot/detail_15.html)
  - [Tohoku Kizuna Matsuri](https://tohoku-kizunamatsuri.jp/special/en/learn/aomori)
- Sound: the festival band (Nebuta hayashi) plays taiko drums, hand cymbals and flutes; described from the voice note supplied.
- Floats: wire frame, washi paper, hand-painted, lit from inside; made by nebuta masters over months; up to about 9 m wide.
  - [MLIT: Building a Nebuta](https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/en/R1-01048.html)
  - [Nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/views/b01401/)

The sound and the float photo were supplied for this project.
